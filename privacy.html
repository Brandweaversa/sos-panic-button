Privacy Policy

SOS Panic Button — Powered by Brandweaver

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

SOS Panic Button is a free safety app that lets you alert your trusted contacts with your live location in an emergency. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your data is handled.

This app does NOT collect, sell, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Location Data

When location data is collected:

  • Only when you trigger an SOS (by holding the volume button for 3 seconds)
  • Only during an active live tracking session
  • Stops the moment you (or a receiver) tap "Stop"

Who receives your location:

  • Only the phone numbers you manually add to your "Receivers" list in Settings
  • Receivers can view your live position on a map during the active SOS session

Your location is NEVER sent to Brandweaver, never stored on our servers, and never shared with advertisers.

Firebase Anonymous Authentication

The app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to secure the real-time database. This generates a random, anonymous ID for your device.

  • The ID is not linked to your name, phone number, or email
  • The ID resets when you uninstall the app
  • It cannot be used to identify you personally

Phone Number

The app stores your phone number and your receivers' phone numbers locally on your device, using Android's secure SharedPreferences.

  • Phone numbers are used only to route SOS alerts to the correct receivers
  • They are stored only on your device — never uploaded to Brandweaver
  • They are removed when you uninstall the app

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following locally on your device:

  • Your name (so receivers know who's in trouble)
  • Your phone number
  • Your receivers' phone numbers
  • SOS event history (timestamps, trigger source, location)
  • App preferences (Receiver Mode on/off, monitoring on/off)

All of this data is removed when you uninstall the app.

What We DON'T Do

  • No analytics — we don't track how you use the app
  • No advertising — the app is ad-free
  • No third-party tracking — no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no ad networks
  • No data selling — your data is never sold or shared
  • No background location tracking — location is only collected during active SOS
  • No SMS access — the app uses data only, not SMS

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete your data: Uninstall the app — all local data is removed
  • Stop sharing location: Tap "Stop" on the live tracking screen, or simply don't trigger an SOS
  • Remove receivers: Go to Settings → swipe to remove any receiver
  • Disable the app: Toggle off "Receiver Mode" and "Activate monitoring" in the app

Children's Privacy

This app is rated for users aged 12 and above. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 12. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
